The Great Lakes State

Michigan is a state in the Great Lakes region of the upper Midwestern United States. It is uniquely composed of two peninsulas—the Lower Peninsula and the Upper Peninsula—separated by the Straits of Mackinac, a five-mile channel that joins Lake Huron to Lake Michigan.

Key Highlights and Trivia About Michigan

  • Freshwater Coastline: Michigan has the longest freshwater coastline of any political subdivision in the United States, bordering four of the five Great Lakes.
  • The Automotive Capital: Detroit, Michigan's largest city, is globally recognized as the historic heart of the American automotive industry.
  • Two Peninsulas Connected by One Bridge: The Mackinac Bridge, opened in 1957, spans five miles to link the Upper and Lower Peninsulas.
  • Abundant Inland Lakes: Wherever you stand in Michigan, you are never more than six miles away from a natural water source or more than 85 miles from a Great Lake shoreline.
