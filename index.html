Overview of Da Nang

Da Nang is a major coastal city in central Vietnam known for its sandy beaches, vibrant culinary culture, and rich history. Positioned between the UNESCO World Heritage sites of Hue and Hoi An, it serves as an essential cultural and technological hub in Southeast Asia.

Top Five Must-Visit Landmarks

Visitors traveling to Da Nang should explore these famous destinations:

  • The Golden Bridge (Cau Vang): A famous pedestrian bridge held aloft by two giant stone hands at Ba Na Hills.
  • The Marble Mountains (Ngu Hanh Son): A cluster of five marble and limestone hills featuring caves, tunnels, and Buddhist sanctuaries.
  • My Khe Beach: A 30-kilometer stretch of fine white sand stretching from the base of the Son Tra Peninsula.
  • The Dragon Bridge (Cau Rong): A modern architectural marvel shaped like a golden dragon that breathes fire and water on weekend nights.
  • Son Tra Peninsula (Monkey Mountain): A lush nature reserve home to the endangered Red-shanked Douc Langur and the towering Lady Buddha statue.
